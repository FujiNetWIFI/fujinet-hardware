# FujiNet-SMS Rev0: floorplan study (2026-10-08)

The board was laid out fresh and its schematic redrawn to mirror it, so the floorplan was settled
first. Candidates are generated by `tools/placement.py` (`PLACE_VARIANT`), placed by
`tools/gen_pcb.py`, scored by `tools/plot_placement.py`, and then trial-routed: Freerouting
2.4.1, 8 passes, `-us hybrid`, after the RP2354B core-corner pre-route, one scratch copy each,
run in parallel.

## The fact the study starts from

The SMS cartridge edge is the JEDEC 32-pin memory pinout, unrolled.

- **Fingers, west to east:**
  - A15, A12;
  - A7 A6 A5 A4 A3 A2 A1 A0;
  - D0 D1 D2, GND GND, D3 D4 D5 D6 D7;
  - /CE, A10, A11 A9 A8, A13 A14;
  - the strobes.
- **AS6C4008 TSOP-I, in cyclic order:** A12 A7..A0, DQ0-2, VSS, DQ3-7, CE#, A10, OE# | A11 A9 A8 ...

An SRAM standing upright in the classic ROM spot (rotation 270, its pin 17-32 end toward the
fingers, x = 107.6) therefore takes every console line it needs, A0-A12, D0-D7 and GND, without
one crossing.
- A3-A0, D0-D7 and A10 run straight up a funnel into the pin 17-32 row.
- A4-A7 and A12 wrap round the west corner, A8 A9 A11 round the east corner.
- Every B.Cu finger jogs half a pitch east to a via.
- Only CE# (SA19) and OE# are trapped inside the fan; each takes a short diagonal to a via.

`gen_pcb.sram0_fanin()` draws all of this locked, before any router runs; see the close-up in
`floorplan/sram0-fanin.png`.

The RP2354B's GPIO map is fixed by the firmware (`sms_cart.h`: A0-A15 on GPIO0-15, D0-D7 on
16-23, strobes on 24-31). Around the package its cyclic order runs A0→A15 then D0→D7. The
fingers run A7…A0 then D0…D7, so one of the RP's two groups is always reversed against them
and has to go through the inner layers. That holds wherever the RP sits. The RP keeps its proven
macro: rotation 90, the NES / first-SMS decoupling ring, the core-regulator corner and the
pre-route order (`placement.RP_MACRO`). It can be turned as a whole, but every candidate here
uses 90.

## Why P3

- **Routability.** The ROM-spot floorplans beat the first layout from the second pass on, and P3
  led P1 by the fourth. The airwire numbers point the other way: crossing counts ignore layers,
  and the ROM spot's whole gain is on F.Cu.
- **The schematic.** On the page (the board turned so the fingers face left) P3 reads straight
  across: the edge, then SRAM0 in its ROM spot, then the RP2354B. SRAM1 sits above, the glue
  below. P1 put the RP up in a corner of the page.
- **Short glue paths.** The glue sits over the east fingers that drive it (/RD /WR /MREQ /CE A13
  A14) and next to the RP's strobe side. Its /OE, /WE and chip select reach both SRAMs across
  one band.
- **The FujiNet half.** It keeps the top edge.
  - The S3's antenna sits top-left over its 48 mm keep-out. That keep-out bars footprints, and
    `check_parts_inside` now checks it; the microSD moved east of x 99 because of it.
  - The microSD and USB-C mouths are on the top edge.
  - The buttons, LEDs and debug header run down the west side, under the shell's pinholes and
    windows; the label goes east of them.
