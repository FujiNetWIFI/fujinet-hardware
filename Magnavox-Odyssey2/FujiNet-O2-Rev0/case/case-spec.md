# FujiNet-O2 Rev0 shell: dimension dossier

`FujiNet-O2-Shell.scad` is a two-piece clamshell for the 63 x 85 mm board. It holds the board the way bwack's and PicoPAC's boards are meant to be held:
- posts through the two 5.0 mm holes;
- keys through the two side notches.

The bottom 17 mm of the board stays bare, to enter the console's slot. `board-anchors.scad` is generated by `tools/gen_pcb.py` from `tools/placement.py` and `tools/edge_geom.py`, so the openings, keys and posts follow the board.

**No original cartridge or console was measured for this shell.**
- An original Odyssey 2 / Videopac cartridge is reportedly 127 x 80 x 21 mm (ConsoleMods' cartridge-dimension table, seen only in a search snippet).
- PicoPAC's printed shell (aotta/PicoPAC `3dPrint/`, measurements only: the project has no licence) is 80.2 x 134.5 x 20.5 mm. Its bosses sit at x +-27 mm, 25 mm above the contact edge, matching the board's holes. Its README calls it a work in progress.
- PicoPAC's photos show its bare board standing in a G7000's slot with the parts toward the player.

So the shell's size comes from the board. **VERIFY** marks every number that stands in for a measurement nobody has made. Check each one before printing more than one shell.

## The shell

| | |
|---|---|
| Outside | 70.0 x 71.5 x 13.6 mm (W x H x D):<br>• width: the 63 mm board + 2 x (2 wall + 1 lip + 0.5 clearance). An original is 80 mm wide, so the shell fits wherever one does;<br>• height: from 17 mm above the contact edge to 3.5 mm above the board's top edge;<br>• depth: 2 + 3.0 + 1.6 + 5.0 + 2. |
| Front half | Over F.Cu: every part, and pins 1-15. In a G7000 that face is toward the player (PicoPAC's photos). It has:<br>• RESET through a 4.8 mm hole, with a printed plunger;<br>• BOOTSEL as a 2.2 mm pin-hole (no guide tube: RESET's guide is 6.4 mm away);<br>• S3 EN and S3 BOOT as 2.2 mm pin-holes with guide tubes (a straightened paper clip);<br>• the WS2812 and RP-LED windows (3.0 / 2.0 mm), in shrouds;<br>• a 44 x 14 mm label recess, 0.4 mm deep, under the RP. |
| Back half | Over B.Cu (pins A-R, no parts), toward the back of the console. It has:<br>• a 54 x 46 mm label recess, 0.4 mm deep, above the screw heads;<br>• the 1 mm alignment lip, rising into the front half. |
| Top wall | • USB-C opening 13 x 7.2 mm, sized for a plug's overmold.<br>• Plastic only over the ESP32-S3's antenna. |
| Left wall | microSD slot 14 x 2.4 mm on the card's centreline (y 62.7, between the TF-015's pegs). In the front half the slot is open up to the face, so a thumb reaches the latched card. |
| Bottom | Closed walls with a slot for the full-width board; 17 mm of board stands out below (VERIFY: how deep the console's slot takes it). The front half's bottom wall rests on F.Cu across the parts-free band (20 mm), the back half's on B.Cu. |
| Holding the board | • At the two posts: Ø8.6 front collars on F.Cu (no part within 4.5 mm of a hole), their Ø4.6 spigots through the 5.0 mm holes into sockets in the back bosses.<br>• Rectangular keys from the back half, through the side notches, into the front half's ribs.<br>• 1.2 mm ribs on F.Cu along the sides' lower part (from y 89 on the left, below BOOTSEL; from y 85 on the right, below the buck). Higher up, parts reach the edges.<br>• On B.Cu: a ledge round the board, and a pad under each button. |
| Fasteners | • 2x M2.5x10 pan head from the back half, through the back bosses, into the spigots' 2.1 mm self-tap pilots.<br>• Two snap hooks hold the top edge, where the board has no holes. |

Export:

```
openscad -D 'part="front"'   -o front.stl   case/FujiNet-O2-Shell.scad
openscad -D 'part="back"'    -o back.stl    case/FujiNet-O2-Shell.scad
openscad -D 'part="plunger"' -o plunger.stl case/FujiNet-O2-Shell.scad
openscad -D 'part="both"'    -o both.stl    case/FujiNet-O2-Shell.scad   # all three, 152 x 72 mm (FujiNet-O2-Shell.stl)
openscad -D 'part="check"'         -o check.stl case/FujiNet-O2-Shell.scad   # must report an empty object
openscad -D 'part="parts_check"'   -o pc.stl    case/FujiNet-O2-Shell.scad   # must report an empty object
openscad -D 'part="parts_control"' -o ctl.stl   case/FujiNet-O2-Shell.scad   # must NOT be empty
```

`part="assembled"` and `part="section"` (cut through the posts) are for viewing.

**Printing:**
- Each half prints outside-down, with no supports, at 0.2 mm layers, in PETG or PLA. The plunger prints cap-down.
- The front half is turned over for printing by a rotation, never a `mirror()`: a mirror would export the half's mirror image, which does not fit the board.
- No carbon-fibre or metal-filled filament: the ESP32-S3's antenna is behind the top wall.
- The label recesses face the bed, so the layer above each one prints as a bridge. The label covers it. Set `label_t = 0` to leave them out.

**Assembly:**
1. Drop the plunger, cap first, into the front half's RESET guide.
2. Lay the board in the back half, B.Cu down: its notches over the keys, its holes over the bosses.
3. Close the front half from the top edge down. The hooks snap in, and the spigots enter the board's holes.
4. Fit the two screws from the back.

**The SCAD asserts:**
- the board fits the cavity, and the tallest part (3.26 mm, the USB-C) clears the front plate;
- the bottom wall sits in the parts-free band, below the notch keys, and clear of the contacts;
- no screw within 15 mm of the antenna;
- the RESET guide clears the BOOTSEL hole, and the S3 pin-hole tubes clear each other;
- the labels clear the face holes and the screw heads;
- the hooks clear the USB-C opening;
- the side ribs clear every part's courtyard, and the post collars stay inside the parts-free rings.

**Checks run on 2026-10-30:**
- `part="check"`: empty. The board and the plunger only touch the shell.
- `part="parts_check"`: empty. Every F.Cu courtyard (117 parts, from `board-anchors.scad`), extruded to its part's height, clears both halves.
- `part="parts_control"`: not empty. The same boxes raised 2.5 mm meet the front plate, so the check can fail.

## Before printing more than one

Fit a printed shell in a console and check:
1. **The insertion depth.** The board must reach the connector before the shell's bottom meets anything. If it doesn't, raise `board_out`; the parts-free band allows up to 18 mm.
2. **The well.** The shell is 70 x 13.6 mm in plan, inside an original's 80 x 21. Check that the console's slot takes it, and that the USB-C (top) and the microSD (left side, 67 mm above the contact edge) stay reachable with the cart in.
3. **The faces.** The 1-15 face should face the player (as in PicoPAC's photos), so the label face matches an original's.
