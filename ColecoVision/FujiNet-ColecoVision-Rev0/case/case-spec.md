# FujiNet-ColecoVision Rev0 shell: dimension dossier

`FujiNet-ColecoVision-Shell.scad` is a two-piece clamshell for the 40.2 x 82.4 mm board. `board-anchors.scad` is
generated by `tools/gen_pcb.py` from `tools/placement.py` and `tools/edge_geom.py`, so the openings, keys and ribs
follow the board.

**No original cartridge, shell or console was measured for this shell.** Its outside is a reproduction shell's:
- aotta's PiCOLECO printed shell (GPL-3.0; its STLs measured only, nothing copied): 82.0 x 97.0 x 19.0 mm, two halves
  (13.5 / 11 mm, overlapping), a 2.5 mm back, a 2.0 mm front, cross ribs whose tops (6.1 mm off the back) carry the
  board's back face, side ribs at +-20 mm round the 40.6 mm board, the board's top edge at the top wall and its
  fingers ~12 mm inside the open bottom, an opening in the top wall;
- sparkletron's multicart shell (MIT): 82.55 x 99.06 x 22.86 mm.

**VERIFY** marks every number that stands in for a measurement nobody has made. Check each one before printing more
than one shell.

Export:

```
openscad -D 'part="front"'   -o front.stl   case/FujiNet-ColecoVision-Shell.scad
openscad -D 'part="back"'    -o back.stl    case/FujiNet-ColecoVision-Shell.scad
openscad -D 'part="plunger"' -o plunger.stl case/FujiNet-ColecoVision-Shell.scad
openscad -D 'part="both"'    -o both.stl    case/FujiNet-ColecoVision-Shell.scad   # all three (FujiNet-ColecoVision-Shell.stl)
openscad -D 'part="check"'         -o check.stl case/FujiNet-ColecoVision-Shell.scad   # must report an empty object
openscad -D 'part="parts_check"'   -o pc.stl    case/FujiNet-ColecoVision-Shell.scad   # must report an empty object
openscad -D 'part="parts_control"' -o ctl.stl   case/FujiNet-ColecoVision-Shell.scad   # must NOT be empty
```

`part="assembled"` and `part="section"` (cut across the RP) are for viewing.

**Printing:**
- Each half prints outside-down, with no supports, at 0.2 mm layers, in PETG or PLA. The plunger prints cap-down.
- The front half is turned over for printing by a rotation, never a `mirror()`: a mirror would export the half's
  mirror image, which does not fit the board.
- No carbon-fibre or metal-filled filament: the ESP32-S3's antenna is behind the west wall.

**Assembly:**
1. Drop the plunger, cap first, into the front half's RESET guide.
2. Lay the board in the back half, B.Cu down: its notches over the keys.
3. Close the front half.
4. Fit the four screws from the back.

**The SCAD asserts:**
- the board fits the cavity, and the tallest part (3.26 mm, the USB-C) clears the front plate;
- the skirt is at least 10 mm, and no notch key reaches into the connector's space;
- no screw within 15 mm of the antenna strip, and no boss on the board;
- the RESET guide clears the S3 BOOT hole;
- the labels clear the face holes and the screw heads;
- the side ribs clear every part's courtyard.

**Checks run on 2026-11-05 (the routed board):**
- `part="check"`: empty. The board and the plunger only touch the shell.
- `part="parts_check"`: empty. Every F.Cu courtyard (from `board-anchors.scad`), extruded to its part's height,
  clears both halves.
- `part="parts_control"`: not empty. The same boxes raised to the front plate meet it, so the check can fail.

## Before printing more than one

Fit a printed shell in a console and check:
1. **The insertion depth.** The fingers must seat in the connector before the shell's skirt bottoms out. If they
   don't, shorten `shell_h` (the skirt), keeping `skirt >= 10`.
2. **The connector's body** must fit the skirt's cavity (78 x 14.5 mm inside) and stay below board y 120.5.
3. **The faces.** The component (even-pin) face should face the label side of an original cart.

## An original shell

The board's outline is the open carts' (width, notches), so an original shell's ribs should take it, but the
82.4 mm length is proven only in printed shells, and an original's interior above the notches (posts, a cross rib)
has not been measured. ThatOldNerd's 56.5 mm board has two 3.4 mm holes 50.5 mm up; this board has none there (the
ESP32-S3 module sits on that spot). Before fitting one: measure the shell's interior length from where the fingers
sit to the top wall (at least 83 mm, plus the USB-C plug's reach), look for posts or ribs above 43 mm, then cut the
top wall for the USB-C (13 x 7.2 mm, its centre 1.63 mm above F.Cu) and the microSD (14 x 2.4 mm, from 0.2 mm
above F.Cu), at the x positions `board-anchors.scad` gives (`usb_c`, `microsd`).
