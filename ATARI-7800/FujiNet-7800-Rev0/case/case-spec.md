# FujiNet-7800 Rev0 shell: dimension dossier

`FujiNet-7800-Shell.scad` is a two-piece clamshell built around a 7800 cartridge's **base**:
- the width and depth the console's slot takes;
- the board centred in the depth;
- the contact edge recessed inside an open connector mouth.

It is grown **taller** to hold the 72.4 x 115 mm FujiNet board, the user's choice over squeezing
the board into a stock shell. `board-anchors.scad` is generated by `tools/gen_pcb.py` from
`tools/placement.py`, so the openings follow the board.

Every number below has its source. **VERIFY** marks a number that was not measured on a real 7800
cartridge or console; check each one before printing more than one shell.

## The shell

| | |
|---|---|
| Outside | 80.0 x 125.0 x 20.0 mm (W x H x D). |
| Label half | Over the component side (F.Cu). It carries every part and pins 1-16, and faces the **console's rear**, the way a 7800 cartridge's label faces ("hold the cartridge so the name on the label faces away from you", 7800 manual). It has:<br>• a 54 x 38 mm label recess, 0.4 mm deep;<br>• RESET through a 6.2 mm hole with a printed plunger;<br>• BOOTSEL, S3 EN and S3 BOOT as 2.2 mm pin-holes;<br>• WS2812 and RP-LED windows (3.0 / 2.0 mm) near the top edge;<br>• the microSD finger notch. |
| Back half | A flat plate over B.Cu (no parts), toward the player. It has screw counterbores and a 1 mm alignment lip into the label half. |
| Top wall | USB-C opening 13 x 7.2 mm, sized for a plug overmold. microSD slot 14 x 2.4 mm. |
| Bottom | The connector mouth, 57 mm wide through the full inner depth (VERIFY). The contact edge sits 6.5 mm up inside it (VERIFY). The console's two plastic keys enter the board's key slots inside the mouth. |
| Fasteners | 4x M3x12 pan head from the back half, through the board, into the label half's bosses. Use 2.6 mm self-tap pilots, or 4.0 mm for heat-set inserts with M3x10. |
| Parting plane | The board's B.Cu face, at the middle of the depth (VERIFY: the console's connector centre). |

Export:

```
openscad -D 'part="label"'   -o label.stl   case/FujiNet-7800-Shell.scad
openscad -D 'part="back"'    -o back.stl    case/FujiNet-7800-Shell.scad
openscad -D 'part="plunger"' -o plunger.stl case/FujiNet-7800-Shell.scad
openscad -D 'part="check"'   -o check.stl   case/FujiNet-7800-Shell.scad   # must report an empty object
```

The SCAD asserts:
- the width is no more than a 2600 cartridge's;
- the board clears the walls and the lip;
- the tallest part (3.3 mm) clears the label plate, with a 7.2 mm gap;
- the mouth is wider than the tab and narrower than the cavity.

`part="check"` intersects the board with both halves. It must be empty: the board only touches
the bosses.

| Item | Value | Source |

## VERIFY before relying on it

1. **Fit the edge first. This gates ordering the board.**
   - Print `docs/layout-front.svg` at 1:1, or order a cheap 2-layer coupon of the tab.
   - Check that the key slots take the console's keys and that every finger lands on its contact. Try the fit in a 7800 (NTSC and, if available, PAL).
   - Lay it over a real 7800 cartridge board.
2. **Board thickness.** Measure an original. Real carts measure 1.5 mm, and 1.6 mm fits.
3. **Shell width and depth.** 80 x 20 mm is chosen to sit inside what a 2600 cartridge (81.5 x 20.2 mm) occupies, since the 7800's slot takes 2600 cartridges. Measure a 7800 cartridge and the slot opening.
4. **Board position in the depth.** The board is centred. Check it against the console's connector, which must take the board without the shell rubbing a side of the slot.
5. **Edge recess (6.5 mm) and mouth (57 mm).**
   - The console's connector housing must enter the mouth.
   - The fingers must seat fully before the shell meets the slot surround.
   - The two keys must reach the board's key slots.
6. **Width just above the tab.** The board is 72.4 mm wide from 16.5 mm above the edge. Otaku-flash proves about 54-58 mm at that height. Check that nothing in the console meets the 72.4 mm body.
7. **Height.** The shell rises about 125 mm, against a stock cartridge's roughly 100. Check that nothing on the console top (the switches) is in the way.
8. **Insertion depth vs the bring-up pads.** The test-pad block sits 68-71 mm above the edge (pad rows at y 81.6 / 79.0, edge at y 150). It must be outside the console when a bare board is plugged in for bring-up.
