# FujiNet-StudioII Rev0 shell: dimension dossier

`FujiNet-StudioII-Shell.scad` is a two-piece clamshell for the 90.9 x 111.5 mm board. The board is
FliP's Studio II multicart outline grown 28 mm taller for the ESP32-S3 half, so an original shell no
longer fits.

The shell holds the board the way an original holds FliP's:
- posts through his two 8.128 mm holes;
- keys in his two side notches, where the original's rivets go.

The bottom 15 mm of the board stays bare, to enter the console's slot. `board-anchors.scad` is
generated by `tools/gen_pcb.py` from `tools/placement.py`, so the openings follow the board.

**No original cartridge or console was measured for this shell, and no size is published.**
- Searched 2026-10-11: CHM's catalogue, FliP's repository, AtariAge, cosmacvip.com, nicole.express.
- Thingiverse 4890690, a printed Studio II shell, could not be fetched.

So the shell's size comes from the board. **VERIFY** marks every number that stands in for a
measurement nobody has made. Check each one before printing more than one shell.

Export:

```
openscad -D 'part="front"'   -o front.stl   case/FujiNet-StudioII-Shell.scad
openscad -D 'part="back"'    -o back.stl    case/FujiNet-StudioII-Shell.scad
openscad -D 'part="plunger"' -o plunger.stl case/FujiNet-StudioII-Shell.scad
openscad -D 'part="both"'    -o both.stl    case/FujiNet-StudioII-Shell.scad   # all three, 208 x 100 mm
openscad -D 'part="check"'   -o check.stl   case/FujiNet-StudioII-Shell.scad   # must report an empty object
```

`part="assembled"` and `part="section"` (cut through the posts) are for viewing.

**Printing:**
- Each half prints outside-down, with no supports, at 0.2 mm layers, in PETG or PLA. The plunger
  prints cap-down.
- The front half is turned over for printing by a rotation. A `mirror([0, 0, 1])` would export the
  half's mirror image, which does not fit the board.
- No carbon-fibre or metal-filled filament: the ESP32-S3's antenna is behind the top wall.
- The label recesses face the bed, so the layer above each one prints as a bridge. The label covers
  it. Set `label_t = 0` to leave them out.

**Assembly:**
1. Drop the plunger, cap first, into the front half's RESET guide.
2. Lay the board in the back half, parts up: its notches over the keys, its holes over the bosses.
3. Close the front half from the top edge down. The hooks snap in, and the spigots enter the board's
   holes.
4. Fit the two screws from the back.

**The SCAD asserts:**
- the board fits the cavity;
- the tallest part (3.3 mm) clears the front plate with a 4.1 mm gap, and a fitted debug header
  (8.5 mm) stays inside the front face;
- the bottom wall sits in the parts-free band, below the notch keys, and clear of the contacts;
- no screw within 15 mm of the antenna;
- the RESET guide clears the pin-hole tubes;
- the labels clear the face holes and the screw heads;
- the hooks clear the openings.

**Checks run on 2026-10-11:**
- `part="check"`: empty. The board and the plunger only touch the shell.
- Every F.Cu courtyard (108 parts), extruded to its part's height and intersected with both halves:
  empty. A positive control, with the parts raised 4 mm, does intersect.
- The openings sit on their parts:
  - RESET, BOOTSEL, S3 EN and S3 BOOT on SW2, SW1, SW3 and SW4;
  - the LED windows on D3 and D1;
  - the debug window on J2's three pins;
  - the USB-C opening on J4's pegs, the microSD slot on J3's.
- Cross-sections reviewed: the posts, the notches, the button row, the top wall (USB-C, hooks,
  microSD), the bottom wall, the sides.

## VERIFY before relying on it

1. **The bare board below the shell (15 mm, `board_out`).**
   - Plug a bare board into a console and mark how far it goes in before the contacts seat.
   - The shell's bottom must stay above the console's top, and above anything round the slot.
   - The limits are 9.55 mm (the contacts' mask) and 23.5 mm (the parts-free band).
2. **The width and depth (97.9 x 16 mm) against the slot's surroundings.** Nothing near the slot on
   the console's top may meet the shell, in front of the board or behind it.
3. **The board's depth in the shell (`gap_b` 3.0, `gap_f` 7.4).**
   - The board sits 5 mm from the back face and 9.4 mm from the front face.
   - If the console needs the shell centred on the board, or the original's offset, change
     `gap_b` / `gap_f`. The asserts keep the parts clear.
4. **The height.** The shell is 100 mm tall, and the board is 28 mm taller than an original's. The
   slot is open above; check that nothing else on or around the console is in the way.
5. **The fits, after one test print.** Adjust the parameters in brackets:
   - the spigots in the board's holes: 7.8 mm in 8.128 (`spig_d`);
   - the keys in the notches: 0.15 mm clearance (`key_clr`);
   - the snap hooks: 0.45 mm engagement (`barb`);
   - the plunger: 0.6 mm proud of the face, 0.15 mm above the switch at rest (`proud`,
     `press_gap`).
6. **USB-C plugs.** The opening runs 3.0 mm in front of the receptacle's face. A plug's overmold must
   reach into it to mate fully. Check the cables you use.
