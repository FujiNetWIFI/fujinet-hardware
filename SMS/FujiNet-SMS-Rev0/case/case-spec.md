# FujiNet-SMS Rev0 shell: dimension dossier

`FujiNet-SMS-Shell.scad` is a two-piece clamshell built around a Sega Master
System cartridge's **base**. The base is the part the slot sees: 109.1 mm wide,
16.9 mm deep, the board seated as in the original, and the contact edge inside
an open connector mouth. It is grown **taller**, 100.9 mm instead of
69.3 mm, to hold the 100 mm-wide FujiNet board. `board-anchors.scad` is
generated by `tools/gen_pcb.py` from `tools/placement.py`, so the openings
follow the board.

Every number below has its source. **VERIFY** marks a number that was not
measured on a real cartridge or console; check each one before printing more
than one shell.

## The shell

| | |
|---|---|
| Outside | 109.1 x 100.9 x 16.9 mm (W x H x D). The original is 109.1 x 69.3 x 16.9. |
| Front half | The label face, over the component side (F.Cu). It carries every part and the even fingers. It has: a 74 x 36 mm label recess (0.4 mm deep, lower face, clear of the buttons and LEDs); RESET through a 6.2 mm hole with a printed plunger; BOOTSEL / S3 EN / S3 BOOT as 2.2 mm pin-holes; WS2812 and RP-LED windows (3.0 / 2.0 mm); the microSD finger notch. |
| Rear half | A flat back plate over B.Cu (no parts). It has screw counterbores and a 1 mm alignment lip into the front half. |
| Top wall | USB-C opening 13 x 7.2 mm, sized for a plug overmold. microSD slot 14 x 2.4 mm. |
| Bottom | The connector mouth: 76 mm wide through the full inner depth (VERIFY). The contact edge sits 5.4 mm up inside it. |
| Fasteners | 4x M3x12 pan head from the back, through the board, into the front bosses: 2.6 mm self-tap pilots, or 4.0 mm for heat-set inserts with M3x10. |
| Parting plane | The board's B.Cu face. The board rests on the rear bosses and is clamped by the front bosses. |

Export:

```
openscad -D 'part="front"'   -o front.stl   case/FujiNet-SMS-Shell.scad
openscad -D 'part="rear"'    -o rear.stl    case/FujiNet-SMS-Shell.scad
openscad -D 'part="plunger"' -o plunger.stl case/FujiNet-SMS-Shell.scad
```

Print each half outside down, with no supports, 0.2 mm layers, in PETG or PLA. The SCAD asserts:
- the base stays 109.1 mm wide;
- the board fits the cavity;
- the tallest part (3.3 mm) clears the front plate, with a 6.3 mm gap;
- the mouth is wider than the tab.

## The original cartridge (sources)

| Item | Value | Source / trust |
|---|---|---|
| Shell outside | 109.1 W x 69.3 H x 16.9 D mm | bsittler, digital caliper (SMS Power forum thread 17153). Good. |
| Board | ~66.0 x 40.1 mm, plain rectangle, fingers across the full width | MrSVCD 1200 dpi scan of a 171-5519 (thread 17935). Scan, +/-0.3 mm. |
| Board seat | component face 8.3 mm inside the label face | MrSVCD R2 replica shell (OpenSCAD, CC BY-SA 4.0). Replica, not measured. VERIFY. |
| Edge recess | contact edge ~5.4 mm up inside the shell bottom | inferred from the replica SCAD and the scan. Low trust. VERIFY. |
| Narrow section | a cross-rib 15 mm up with a 66 mm board cut-out | replica SCAD. VERIFY; this board keeps 65.8 mm for its first 15 mm. |
| Retention | two screws on the back | little-scale; replica: 2 x 3.6 x 16 countersunk at +/-42 mm, 35 mm up |

## VERIFY before relying on it

1. **Board thickness.** Measure an original with calipers. 1.2 mm would need `pcb_t` and the board stack changed.
2. **Edge recess and mouth.**
   - Check that the console's connector housing enters the 76 mm mouth.
   - Check that the fingers seat fully before the shell meets the slot surround.
   - Try SMS1 (dust flap), SMS2 and the Genesis + Power Base Converter.
3. **Tab depth.** Check that nothing on the console side meets the board where it widens, 15 mm above the edge.
4. **Seat depth.** With the board 8.3 mm inside the label face, the fingers are centred in the original's position. A console with a tight slot will show it.
5. **Overlay.** Overlay a 1:1 print of the tab (`docs/layout-front.svg`) on a real cartridge board before ordering.
6. **Height.** The shell is 31.6 mm taller than an original. The SMS1 and SMS2 slots are open above, and on the Genesis it sits on the Power Base Converter. Check that no lid or dust cover is in the way on the consoles you use.
